## Overview
A clean, multi-purpose online store landing page suitable for fashion, electronics, home goods, and lifestyle products.

## Features
- Hero slider with promotional offers
- Trust badges (Quality Product, Free Shipping, 14-Day Return, 24/7 Support)
- Category grid with product counts
- Featured products section with pricing and ratings
- Special offer banners
- Recent products section
- Partner/brand logo strip
- Newsletter signup
- Detailed footer with contact info, quick links, account links, and newsletter

## Preview Sections
- Top bar with account, currency, and language options
- Navigation menu with categories dropdown
- Hero banner sliders
- Trust/feature icons row
- Category showcase grid
- Featured and recent product grids
- Promotional banners
- Brand partners
- Footer with contact details and social links
